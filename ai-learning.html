Three ideas to start

AI is a capability, not a strategy.

A strong AI program starts with a useful business outcome, reliable information, and clear human ownership.

01

Assist

Use AI to draft, summarize, classify, search, translate, and help people make decisions faster. Humans still approve the outcome.

02

Automate

Connect AI to repeatable workflows. Let it move information between tools, prepare actions, and request approval at the right moment.

03

Autonomize

Give an agent a bounded goal, tools, rules, and an escalation path. Autonomy should be earned through testing and observability.
